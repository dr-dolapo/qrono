Delete your account

You can delete your Qrono account yourself at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Qrono and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete my account.

On the web

  1. Log in at app.qrono.co.uk.
  2. Go to Settings → Your settings.
  3. Under Your account, choose Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

Can’t log in?

Email privacy@qrono.co.uk from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We’ll confirm it’s you, then delete it.

What gets deleted

  • Deleted straight away: your name, email address, password, sessions, devices and notification tokens, Inbox, Archived list and settings.
  • Teams: you leave all of them. Teams where you’re the only member are deleted, with their events.
  • Kept by your teams: programs, comments and approvals you were part of stay with the teams that own them, showing “Deleted user” instead of your name.
  • Backups: deleted data disappears from our backups within 30 days.

If you’re the only Admin of a team that has other people, make someone else an Admin first, so the team isn’t left without one.
