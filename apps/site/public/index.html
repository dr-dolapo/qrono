From first draft to final applause

Agendas need input from several people, change at short notice and have to reach everyone at once. Qrono keeps it all in one place.

  1. Plan together

    Build the program item by item: what happens, for how long and who’s on. Planners draft it; teammates comment on the parts that concern them.

  2. Get it approved

    Send the draft to an Admin, who approves it from the web or their phone. Everyone on the program is told their part and confirms they’ll be there.

  3. Run it live

    Run the event from a command centre on a screen backstage. Each item moves on in time, and every phone stays in step as the program changes.

Everyone in the right place, at the right time

Command centre

A big, clear view of what’s on now, what’s next and how much time is left, for the people on stage and backstage.

Broadcasts

Send a message to everyone on the program mid-event. They can acknowledge it, snooze it for three minutes or mark it done.

On the lock screen

During a live event, the phone’s lock screen shows the latest message and tells people when they’re up next.

An Inbox for what matters

Approvals, parts to confirm and invitations wait in one place, with quick buttons to deal with them.

Guests without the app

Guest speakers get a personal link by email to see their part and confirm it in their phone’s browser.

Reminders

Everyone on the program gets reminder emails before the event, at the times your team chooses.
