Support

We’re here to help with anything in Qrono.

Email support@qrono.co.uk. Tell us what you were doing, what happened and, if you can, add a screenshot. For questions about your personal data, email privacy@qrono.co.uk.

Common questions

How do I create an account?

Sign up at app.qrono.co.uk/signup, or tap “Create an account” in the app. Confirm your email, then create a team or accept an invitation.

I forgot my password

Tap “Forgot password?” on the log-in screen and we’ll email you a link to set a new one.

How do I join my team?

Open the invitation your team sent you by email or link. If you used a shared link, an Admin or Planner will let you in. Invitations to people who already use Qrono also appear in the app’s Inbox.

I’m not getting notifications

In the app, go to Profile → Notifications to check they’re on, and tap “Open phone settings” if not. On iPhone, Live Activities also need to be allowed for Qrono in Settings. On Android 16 and later, turn on Live Updates for Qrono.

Where do things go after I deal with them in my Inbox?

To Archived, at the top right of the Inbox. Archived items are cleared after 60 days.
