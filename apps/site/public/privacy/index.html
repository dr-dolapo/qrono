Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Qrono collects, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. Qrono is made by Tend Technologies Limited (“we”, “us”). For the personal data described here, we are the data controller under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

Questions or requests about your data: privacy@qrono.co.uk

1. What we collect

DataWhere it comes fromWhy we use it
Account details: your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure one-way hash)You, when you sign up or edit your profileTo create your account, log you in and contact you about it
Team details: teams you belong to, your role, invitations you send or receive, and join requestsYou and your teammatesTo run your team and decide what you can see and do
Event content: programs, items, timings, who is assigned to what, comments, approvals, broadcasts and your responses (for example confirming or declining a part, with a reason if you give one)You and your teammatesTo provide Qrono’s planning, approval and live features

Your settings: reminder preferences, Inbox read status and the Archived list of items you’ve dealt withYour use of QronoTo show your Inbox and remember your choices
Security data: session tokens, IP addresses and sign-in attemptsYour device and our serversTo keep you signed in, protect accounts from misuse and limit repeated failed sign-ins
Messages to usYou, when you email supportTo answer you

We do not use advertising trackers or sell personal data, and we don’t use your data to build advertising profiles. Qrono uses one essential cookie on the web to keep you signed in; it is not used for tracking.

2. Our lawful bases

  • Contract: most processing is needed to provide Qrono to you under our Terms.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping Qrono secure, preventing misuse and improving reliability, in ways you would reasonably expect.
  • Consent: push notifications and Live Activities are only sent once you allow them on your phone. You can turn them off at any time in your phone’s settings.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or share data.

3. Data your team controls

Programs, comments, broadcasts and guest details belong to the team that creates them. Admins and Planners decide what goes in them and who is added. If you’re named on a team’s program and have a question about it, please contact that team first; we’ll help if they can’t.

4. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the people and services needed to run Qrono:

  • Your teams: your teammates see your name and email and the parts you’re assigned. Admins and Planners see members’ roles and responses.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosts Qrono’s servers and database and sends our emails.
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.): delivers push notifications and app updates.
  • Apple and Google: deliver notifications and Live Activities to iPhones and Android phones.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it, or to protect people’s safety or our rights.
  • A buyer or successor: if Tend Technologies Limited is sold or merges, under the same protections as this policy.

Each service provider processes data only on our instructions and under contracts that require them to protect it.

5. International transfers

Our service providers run servers in several countries, so your data may be processed outside Nigeria. When it is, we rely on the safeguards the NDPA allows, such as the provider’s contractual commitments to protect personal data to an adequate standard.

6. How long we keep data

  • Your account: until you delete it. When you do, we delete your name, email, password, devices, Inbox and settings straight away. Programs you worked on stay with their teams, showing “Deleted user” in place of your name.
  • Archived list: 60 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Guest links: they stop working after the event. Guest details stay with the team’s program until the team deletes it.
  • Teams: until an Admin deletes the team. A team is also deleted when its only member deletes their account.
  • Backups: our database keeps restore points for up to 30 days, after which deleted data is gone from them too.

7. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your personal data, including in a portable format;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete (you can change your name in the app yourself);
  • delete your data (you can delete your account yourself; see how);
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • withdraw consent you’ve given, without affecting what we did before.

Email privacy@qrono.co.uk. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we’ll reply within the time the law allows. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

8. Security

Connections to Qrono are encrypted. Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes, sign-in attempts are limited, and access to data is restricted to what each role needs. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we’ll tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

9. Children

You must be at least 18 to create a Qrono account. Teams must not add a child’s details to a program without the consent of a parent or guardian. If you think we hold a child’s data without consent, email us and we’ll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we’ll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.

11. Contact

Tend Technologies Limited. Email privacy@qrono.co.uk.
